Model

C(t,r) = 1 − (1−cface(t))(1−chair(r))(1−cbrow(r))(1−cvoice(r)), with cface(t) = 0.15 + 0.55·σ(−0.18(t−16)), chair = 0.60(1−0.85r), cbrow = 0.40(1−0.85r), cvoice = 0.75(1−0.80r). B(t) = B₀(1+βt). ℓ(t) = ℓ₀e−0.03t. The interior optimum solves B(τ)+ℓ(τ) = A·C(τ,r). Full derivation, step discontinuities, sensitivities and limitations are in docs/model.pdf.
